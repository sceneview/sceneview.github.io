class CameraPath(keyframes: List<CameraKeyframe>, val easing: (Float) -> Float = CameraEasing.Expressive, val shortestArc: Boolean = true, val loop: Boolean = false)

A camera move through keyframes: the camera passes each CameraKeyframe.shot at its time and flows between them on a cubic spline (Catmull-Rom over the orbit parameters), so its speed is continuous across a keyframe — no kink, no change of pace the eye can catch.

CameraShotSink
Link copied to clipboard
interface CameraShotSink

A gesture manipulator that can be handed the camera mid-move: where a cinematic stops, the gesture starts from that very shot, at that very velocity, so a drag on a turning camera keeps turning under the finger. Implement it on an orbit manipulator that keeps its own pose and inertia; CinematicCameraManipulator calls it on the first touch of a playing move, when a move ends on its own (with CameraShotVelocity.Zero), and on CameraAnimator.snapTo.
