CameraPosePath

class CameraPosePath : CameraTimeline

A camera move defined by keyframed CameraPoses, sampled with poseAt.

Positions follow interpolation; rotations are interpolated by spherical linear interpolation (slerp) along the shortest arc. Each keyframe quaternion is flipped into the hemisphere of the one before it, so a path never spins the long way round between two keyframes whose quaternions have opposite signs. When lookAt is set, the rotation is derived from the interpolated position instead, so the camera keeps that point at the centre of the frame at every instant, not only on the keyframes.

Deterministic. poseAt is a pure function of its argument: no state, no clock, no frame rate. The same time gives the same pose whatever was sampled before, so a player, a scrubber and an offline renderer stepping at a fixed rate all see the same move.

Time. The path starts at its earliest keyframe and lasts until its latest one (durationSeconds); poseAt counts from that start, like CameraPath.shotAt. Before the start it holds the first pose, after the end the last pose, unless loop wraps time around.

Easing. Each segment is eased by its starting keyframe's CameraPoseKeyframe.easing, or by easing when that is null. A segment eased by CameraEasing.InOut or CameraEasing.Expressive, whose slope is zero at both ends, stops at both of its keyframes; a custom easing stops only where its own slope is zero, and CameraEasing.Linear does not stop. pathEasing, when set, also remaps the whole path's time before the segment is found, so a multi-keyframe move can ease in at the start and out at the end as one gesture. With pathEasing the keyframes are reached at the times the easing maps their own to.

Limits.

  • Rotation is slerped segment by segment (no squad): its direction of turn is continuous but its angular speed can jump at a keyframe. Closer keyframes make the jump smaller.

  • With lookAt, the rotation is rebuilt from up at every instant. A camera that passes straight over or under lookAt (looking along up) flips its roll by 180° at that point; keep the path away from the pole, as orbit does by clamping its elevation.

Experimental. The keyframe types may change with offline rendering; CameraTimeline and CameraPath are not affected.

Ready-made paths: orbit frames a bounding box while turning around it, travelling slides the camera sideways, and dolly moves it toward or away from a target. Play any of them with CameraPathPlayer, or place a camera with io.github.sceneview.node.CameraNode.applyCameraTimeline.
