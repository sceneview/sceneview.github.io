placementBoundsFit

fun placementBoundsFit(boundsCenter: Position, boundsHalfExtent: Size, modelToPlane: Transform, planePolygon: FloatArray): PlacementBoundsFit?

Tests the whole model box against a tracked surface's convex polygon, rather than testing only the hit position or the plane's rectangular extents. Pure JVM geometry.

All eight corners are accounted for, including the height of a tilted box. Only their projection onto plane-local X/Z matters: this checks surface coverage, not support, room height, collisions, or whether the physical surface extends beyond its tracked polygon.

Return

clearance/overhang, or null for missing, nonfinite, degenerate or nonconvex geometry, empty bounds, or a non-affine transform. Flat models are supported.

Parameters

boundsCenter

authored box centre in model-local coordinates (not the hit position).

boundsHalfExtent

nonnegative half-side lengths in the same coordinates.

modelToPlane

affine model-local to plane-local transform, including the placement pose, pivot offsets, asset rotation and final scale. Plane-local +Y is the surface normal.

planePolygon

flat [x0, z0, x1, z1, …] convex polygon in metres, in either winding. Duplicate and collinear vertices are allowed, with 0.00001 m of convexity tolerance for floating-point roundoff.

fun Plane.placementBoundsFit(bounds: Box, modelTransform: Transform): PlacementBoundsFit?

Checks bounds (for example, modelInstance.model.restPoseBoundingBox) at modelTransform against this plane's current polygon, following Plane.getSubsumedBy after merges. modelTransform is the complete model-local to world transform; for an unscaled model at a hit, pass hit.hitPose.transform. For a composed model, pass its node's worldTransform, including its scale and pivot/asset corrections.

Returns null while the plane is not tracking or the geometry has no usable verdict. Boundary contact uses PlacementBoundsFit.fits's numerical tolerance. Re-evaluate as the polygon grows or the object moves/scales. Instant-placement and depth hits have no plane polygon, so they cannot establish a fit. No placement is gated or moved automatically; the caller decides how to present PlacementBoundsFit.fits.
