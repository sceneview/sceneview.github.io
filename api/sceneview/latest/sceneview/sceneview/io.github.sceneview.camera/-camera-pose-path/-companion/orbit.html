orbit

fun orbit(bounds: Aabb, verticalFovDegrees: Double, aspect: Double, durationSeconds: Float = DEFAULT_ORBIT_SECONDS, turns: Float = 1.0f, elevationDegrees: Float = DEFAULT_ORBIT_ELEVATION_DEGREES, startAzimuthDegrees: Float = 0.0f, padding: Float = DEFAULT_FRAMING_PADDING, loop: Boolean = true, keyframesPerTurn: Int = ORBIT_KEYFRAMES_PER_TURN): CameraPosePath

A turn around bounds that keeps the whole box in frame and the box's centre at the centre of the frame.

The distance is computed from the camera's field of view and aspect ratio with fitDistanceForBounds, for the box swept about the vertical axis, so the box fits at every angle of the turn. The camera stays at that distance from the box centre, at elevationDegrees above it, and looks at the centre at every instant (lookAt is set).

Azimuths follow CameraShot: 0° looks from +Z, positive turns go toward +X. Positions are a Catmull-Rom spline through keyframesPerTurn keyframes per turn.

Parameters

bounds

The box to frame, in world space. An empty box is framed at CameraPath.MIN_DISTANCE around its centre, which must be finite: the empty box Filament reports, with infinite corners, has none, so check Aabb.isEmpty first.

verticalFovDegrees

Vertical field of view of the camera that will play the path.

aspect

Viewport width divided by height.

durationSeconds

Length of the whole path. Must be positive.

turns

Number of turns, at most 1000 either way; negative turns the other way. A loop is seamless whatever the number: a whole number of turns ends where it starts, and each lap of a fractional one starts where the last one ended (lap two of 1.5 turns starts half a turn on).

elevationDegrees

Camera elevation above the box centre; positive looks down.

startAzimuthDegrees

Azimuth of the first keyframe.

padding

Extra framing margin, as a fraction of the fitted distance.

loop

Whether the path repeats.

keyframesPerTurn

Keyframes per full turn. At least 6, so consecutive chords stay 60° or less apart and the camera never stops on a keyframe.

Throws

IllegalArgumentException

when durationSeconds is not positive, turns is zero or beyond 1000 either way, verticalFovDegrees or aspect is not positive, or any input (including the bounds) is not finite.
