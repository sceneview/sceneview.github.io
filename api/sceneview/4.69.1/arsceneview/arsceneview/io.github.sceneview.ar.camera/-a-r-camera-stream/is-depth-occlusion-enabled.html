arsceneview/io.github.sceneview.ar.camera/ARCameraStream/isDepthOcclusionEnabled

isDepthOcclusionEnabled

var isDepthOcclusionEnabled: Boolean

Enable the depth occlusion material

This will process the incoming DepthImage to occlude virtual objects behind real world objects.

If the Session is not configured properly the standard camera material is used. Valid Session configuration for the DepthMode are Config.DepthMode.AUTOMATIC and Config.DepthMode.RAW_DEPTH_ONLY

Disable this value to apply the standard camera material to the CameraStream.

Prefer Config.DepthMode.AUTOMATIC: its smoothed depth is dense, where raw depth leaves holes on every untextured surface.

What occludes, and what does not

  • The depth is filtered over time (DepthOcclusionFilter): a frame with no new depth keeps the last one, one-image swings on thin or white occluders are ignored, noise on floors and walls is averaged.

  • The real world must be nearer than the virtual surface by a tolerance that grows with distance (3 cm + 2 % + 1 %/m, so 6 cm at 1 m, 27 cm at 4 m), a hard limit: a model standing on the floor, a shadow lying on it, is never cut by the floor's own noise.

  • Beyond 5 m the real world progressively stops occluding (dithered), and from 8 m it never does: phone depth that far is a guess.

  • Around an object placed with AutoPlacementModel, measured depth up to 10 cm nearer than the surface it stands on counts as that surface: the floor's noise never cuts its contact shadow or its placement ring, and a real object 30 cm in front still hides them.

  • Untextured surfaces (a white wall, a door frame) have little depth of their own: ARCore fills them from their edges, which is right on a flat wall and wrong on a bare one seen from too close. That is a limit of motion-stereo depth, not of this renderer.

Depth buffer lifecycle (#1757)

The ARCore depth com.google.ar.core.Image is copied into the filter and closed in the same frame; Filament uploads from a buffer of our own, recycled by the upload-completed callback. Toggling this property or destroy while an upload is in flight is safe: no ARCore image is held by it.
